1"""End-to-end demo: watch a synthetic manipulation video and learn a skill. 

2 

3The demo: 

41. Renders a short RGB video of the red block being pushed toward the blue block. 

52. Parses the video with a vision parser (Claude vision if ``ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`` 

6 is available, otherwise the deterministic heuristic parser). 

73. Converts the parse result into a ``SkillInstance``. 

84. Verifies the instance with the Chapter 12 physics verifier. 

95. Replays the learned skill and saves it to a JSON skill library. 

10"""

18from chapter16_vision_learning.video_to_skill import replay_skill_in_sim, video_to_skill

20 

21
